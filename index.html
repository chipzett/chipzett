Good to know

What is an agentic note?

A note you can ask and instruct. Switch on Agentic in the write bar and the note gets its own line: ask it a question, or tell it to make a checklist, a summary, a reminder or a web search. The first time, ChipZett asks whether to use AI. If you say no, it answers from the note's own words on your device.

Is the AI free?

Yes, within a daily limit: 10 questions, 3 web searches and 20 voice notes a day. No account and no card.

Is ChipZett free to use?

Yes. There is no payment, no trial and no account. You open it and use it.

Where are my notes saved?

On your own device, inside the browser you are using. They are not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, the notes go with it, so keep a backup: in the app, Appearance has a Download backup button that saves all your notes to one file you can restore later.

Who can see my notes?

Your notes are kept on your device and are not sent to us. The exception is AI: when you ask an agentic note, use Ask your wall, or dictate, that text or recording is sent to an AI service to produce the answer and is not stored by ChipZett. Private and sealed notes are never sent. The privacy page has the details.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It opens in a phone browser, where notes appear as cards you tap to open. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

Can I sync notes across devices?

Not yet. A note written on your laptop stays on your laptop. Optional sync is on the roadmap.

How do I make a note speak?

Press its face. The note uses the voice built into your browser or device. You can turn Sound off in the menu at any time.

Is there an app to install?

You can install ChipZett from your browser today: in Chrome or Edge, open the app and choose Install from the address bar; on a phone, choose Add to Home screen. Store apps for Windows and Android are in development.

How do I send feedback?

Write to chipzett.official@gmail.com. Tell us what felt wrong or what you missed.
