Good to know

Questions people ask first.

Is ChipZett really free?

Yes. There is no payment, no trial and no account. You open it and use it.

Where are my notes saved?

On your own device, inside the browser you are using. They are not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, the notes go with it.

Can anyone else see my notes?

No. Your notes never leave your device, so nobody else can open them, including us.

Will my notes show up on my other devices?

Not yet. A note written on your laptop stays on your laptop. Sign-in and sync across devices are planned.

Does it work on a phone?

It opens in a phone browser, where notes appear as a list. Dragging notes around and resizing them works best on a computer for now.

Do the notes really talk?

Yes. They use the voice built into your browser or computer. You can turn Sound off in the menu at any time.

When is the Windows app coming?

It is being finished. When it is ready, a download button will appear on this page.
