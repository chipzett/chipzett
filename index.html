FAQ

Good to know.

Is ChipZett free to use?

Yes. There is no payment, no trial and no account. You open it and use it.

Where are my notes saved?

On your own device, inside the browser you are using. They are not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, the notes go with it.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Your notes never leave your device, so nobody else can open them, including us.

Is the AI real?

Not yet. Agentic notes are a preview: the note answers from its own words, on your device, with no AI connected. The real agent is planned as a paid plan with an account.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It opens in a phone browser, where notes appear as a list. Moving notes around and resizing them is for computers.

Can I sync notes across devices?

Not yet. A note written on your laptop stays on your laptop. Sign-in and sync are planned.

How do I make a note speak?

Press the speaker on the note. It uses the voice built into your browser or device. You can turn Sound off in the menu at any time.

Can I download the Windows app?

Not yet. It is in development. When it is ready, a download button will appear on this page.

How do I send feedback?

Write to chipzett.official@gmail.com. Tell us what felt wrong or what you missed.
