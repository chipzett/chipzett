Getting started

Your first note in seconds

1. Open ChipZett

It opens in your browser. There is nothing to install.

2. Press Zett

Zett is the green helper in the corner. Use it to write a note or choose a template.

3. Make it yours

Pick a face, a mood and a sound for each note.

Features

What your notes can do

Choose the look and sound of each note, then turn it into a checklist, a reminder or a chart.

Take a stretch break

Read notes aloud

Press a note's face and it reads itself out loud. Choose from nine avatars, including a cat, a robot and an alien.

Set the mood

Pick from 12 animated backgrounds, from falling rain to a burst of confetti.

Add a sound

Give a note its own sound, or put on background music while you work.

Idea→Build→Launch

Turn a line into a visual

Type "chart: Flights 45, Hotel 35, Food 20" for a pie chart, or "flow: Idea > Build > Launch" for a diagram. You can sketch or add a photo too.

Help

Frequently asked questions

Is ChipZett free to use?

Yes. There is no payment, no trial and no account. You open it and use it.

Where are my notes saved?

On your own device, inside the browser you are using. They are not sent to us. If you clear your browser data, the notes go with it.

Who can see my notes?

Only you. Your notes never leave your device, so nobody else can open them, including us.

Can I access my notes on another device?

Not yet. A note written on your laptop stays on your laptop. Optional sync across devices is planned.

Can I use ChipZett on my phone?

Yes, it opens in a phone browser, where notes appear as a list. Moving notes around and resizing them is for computers.

How do I make a note speak?

Press its face. The note uses the voice built into your browser or device. You can turn Sound off in the menu at any time.

When will the Windows app be available?

It is being finished. When it is ready, a download link will appear on this page.

How do I send feedback?

Write to chipzett.official@gmail.com. Tell us what felt wrong or what you missed.

Ready to write your first note?

Open ChipZett in your browser and start writing. It's free, and you don't need an account.
